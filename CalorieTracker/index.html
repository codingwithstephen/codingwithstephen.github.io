Calorie Tracker

Daily, weekly and monthly calorie tracking.

Calorie Tracker lets you log meals from thousands of restaurant, supermarket and packaged foods, set a daily or per-meal calorie target, get meal ideas that fit what you have left, and see your weekly and monthly totals. Your log is stored on your device and syncs across your devices via iCloud.
