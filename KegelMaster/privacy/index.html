KegelMaster Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

KegelMaster does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Your information stays on your device (and in your own iCloud, if enabled). There is no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, and — if you are signed in to iCloud — syncs it privately across your own devices using Apple's iCloud. It is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Your account email and a salted, hashed form of your password (never the password itself).
  • Your training progress: levels, streaks, and session history.
  • Your settings, such as reminders and preferences.

You can erase this at any time from Settings → Delete Account or Settings → Reset all progress, or by deleting the app.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No reminder data is sent to any server.

Subscriptions and payments

KegelMaster Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Payments, billing, and subscription management are handled by Apple. We never receive or store your payment information. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Health disclaimer

KegelMaster provides general wellness guidance and is not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you have pelvic pain, are pregnant, or are recovering from surgery, consult a healthcare professional first.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date.
