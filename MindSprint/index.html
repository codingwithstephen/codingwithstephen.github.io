MindSprint

Quick daily brain workouts.

MindSprint is a few minutes of focused practice a day: five quick games, a Daily Check that gives you a Sprint Score out of 100, and answers you write with your finger. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Apple TV.

Subscription

MindSprint Yearly costs £19.99 per year (or the local equivalent) and starts with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it any time in your Apple ID settings, or in the app under Settings → Manage Subscription.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This removes your sign-in and all your results from the device. Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription — cancel it in your Apple ID settings.
