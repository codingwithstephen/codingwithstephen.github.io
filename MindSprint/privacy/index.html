MindSprint Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

MindSprint does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app records stays on your device. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Sign in with Apple

MindSprint uses Sign in with Apple. Apple gives the app an anonymous identifier and, the first time only, your first name if you choose to share it. Both are stored only on your device (in the Keychain) and are never sent to us. We do not request or store your email address.

Information stored on your device

  • Your game results, Sprint Scores and streaks.
  • Handwriting samples you choose to save in Teach My Writing, used only to recognise your digits on the device.
  • Your settings, such as answer method and writing pause.

Handwriting recognition happens entirely on your device. You can erase everything at any time from Settings → Delete Account, or by deleting the app.

Subscriptions and payments

MindSprint Yearly is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Payments, billing and subscription management are handled by Apple. We never receive or store your payment information. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date.
